Dummy Drop
==========

How to open
-----------
Open the "Dummy Drop" folder on the Desktop and double-click index.html.
The game opens in your browser. No install, no sound.

How to play
-----------
- Drag the yellow crash-test dummy back with the mouse and release to launch him.
- Pulling up and back launches him downward - that is how you hit the low
  glass shelters at ground level.
- You get 5 dummies per level.
- Destroy every pumpkin in the level to clear it. Pumpkins are plain orange
  pumpkins with a green stem - no faces.
- Blocks crack, then break: glass is weakest, wood is in the middle, stone
  almost never breaks. Heavy blocks falling on a pumpkin will squash it.
- Stars are awarded for dummies left over and blocks brought down.

Controls
--------
- Mouse drag and release only.
- Level buttons (1 - House, 2 - Fort, 3 - Keep, 4 - Bridge) jump straight to a level.
- Retry Level restarts the current level. Next Level appears after a win.

Levels
------
1 - House: one pumpkin in a ground shelter under a heavy wood beam.
2 - Fort: two pumpkins, glass posts at two heights with stone above them.
3 - Keep: three pumpkins on three storeys, with a glass plank seam at the base.
4 - Bridge: two pumpkins, one under a stone bridge on glass pillars and one
  on the bridge, with a keep built above it.
